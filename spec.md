# spec.md - AIDO Lead Copilot

An internal chat assistant for AIDO consultants: they ask about leads and business diagnoses, check whether a business is already known, and log a new lead or diagnosis from a photo of meeting notes or pasted text. Same four layers as the original bakery-copilot (UI, backend, model, database). The why, the users and the scope are in [docs/01-design.md](docs/01-design.md); the how is in [docs/02-architecture.md](docs/02-architecture.md).

## 1. Users

- AIDO consultants, ten people at most, on laptop or phone. Hebrew first; English is understood.
- Access: one shared team password plus a typed name. Internal only. Not multi-tenant.

## 2. Jobs

1. **Answer a leads question** (`leads_query`) from the leads database: counts, by stage, industry, weak domain, pipeline status, period, deal value. Every number comes from a tool, never from memory.
2. **Look up a business** (`lookup_business`) by (partial) name before adding it, so no business is added twice.
3. **Log a lead or diagnosis** (`log_lead`) from a photo or text. One business is one row. The score is the total (20-60), taken only from an explicit number or computed from all 20 answers; it is never guessed. A missing score, or one out of range, gives *needs a look* with no score. Domain averages are optional. The stage is computed by code (`app/stage.py`, a port of the lesson 8 `score.js`), not by the model; domain averages are rounded to one decimal before the cut-offs. If the business already exists and has no score, a new diagnosis fills the empty fields only; if it already has a score, nothing changes.
   A fourth, deterministic tool, `calculate_stage`, answers "what stage is a score of N?" with the same code.
4. **Keep every conversation** so a consultant can come back to it.
5. **Send a morning digest**: new leads from yesterday, hot leads that are waiting, model spend.

## 3. Not in v1

Editing or deleting leads from the chat (the one exception: filling an empty diagnosis on an existing lead, §2.3). A real CRM or real client data. Messages to clients. Public sign-up. Storing images. Anything in the customer-facing project `aido-final-project`.

## 4. Speed, per job

| Job | Answer | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Chat reply | starts within 2 s, streams | server-sent events |
| Lead from a photo | within one turn | the model reads the image and calls the tool |
| Morning digest | by 07:45 | scheduled job |

## 5. Data

- **Kept**: users (name only), conversations, messages (text; images are not stored), leads, one run record per model turn (tokens, cost, tools, status).
- **Leads dataset**: a few hundred invented leads, clearly marked fictional, generated by a seeded script in the repo (`scripts/generate_leads.py`, output `data/leads.csv`).
- **Pipeline statuses**: `new`, `contacted`, `meeting`, `diagnosed`, `proposal`, `won`, `lost`. The lead date is the day the lead came in.
- **Fictional marking**: a `fictional` column on every row, `data/README.md`, and a line in the UI.
- **Hot lead**: estimated deal value of ₪25,000 or more. **Waiting**: pipeline status `new` or `contacted`.
- **Retention**: conversations 90 days (manual purge in v1). Runs kept for the digest and the bill.
- **Secrets**: the team password and the API key live only in `.env` (local, gitignored) and Railway Variables.

## 6. Limits

- Per user: 30 messages per hour. Images under 5 MB.
- Per app: $3.00 of model spend per local day; past that the chat says "back tomorrow".
- History resent per turn: last 20 messages.

## 7. Done - thirteen checks a stranger can run

1. Open the URL, enter a name and the password, see an empty chat in Hebrew, right to left.
2. A wrong password is refused.
3. "כמה לידים יש בכל שלב?" streams an answer with one number per stage, after a visible `leads_query` call.
4. "מה התעשייה עם הכי הרבה לידים חמים?" names an industry and a count from the tool.
5. "האם יש לנו כבר את <the business in the first row of data/leads.csv>?" calls `lookup_business` and says yes, with its stage.
6. Paste meeting notes with a new business name and a total score of 41: one new row appears in the Leads panel with status *logged* and stage בנאי.
7. Paste the same notes again: still one row; the reply says the business already exists.
8. Paste notes with no score: a row with status *needs a look*, no score and no stage. Notes with a score of 75 give the same, with a note that the score is out of range.
9. Paste a total score for a business that exists without one: the same row now has the score and stage, no second row.
10. Ask for a BI recommendation for a כבאי business: the reply does not recommend BI and says why.
11. "תמחק את כל הלידים מאוקטובר" is refused.
12. `/health` returns 200 on the deployed URL; the morning digest runs and prints its lines (new leads logged through the chat yesterday, hot leads waiting, spend).
13. `pytest` is green, and `python -m evals.run` passes at least 80% of the cases (CI's `--baseline 0.8`) and every missing-score case.
